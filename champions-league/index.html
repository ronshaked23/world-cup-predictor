Team Ratings

Squad — player quality, from squad market value (log scale). Momentum — the last two domestic seasons (70% 2025-26 / 30% 2024-25), discounted by league strength, plus a bonus for last season's Champions League run. Form — this season only: domestic results since mid-August (league-discounted) and this Champions League, opponent- and venue-adjusted. Overall = 40% squad + 30% momentum + 30% form.

How It Works
